About the ministry

A place for meaning without dogma.

Ministry of Birchwood is a non-denominational ministry rooted in respect for nature, humanity, creativity and the quiet mysteries of life.

We hold space for beginnings, endings, transitions and everything in between through meaningful, inclusive and personal ritual.

Our approach is grounded, compassionate and open to people of any faith or none.

“Ritual is a way of saying this moment matters.”

For people, places and all the in-between.
