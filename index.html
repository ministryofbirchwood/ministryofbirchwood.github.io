About the ministry

A place for meaning without dogma.

Ministry of Birchwood is a non-denominational ministry rooted in respect for nature, humanity, creativity and the quiet mysteries of life.

We hold space for beginnings, endings, transitions and everything in between through meaningful, inclusive and personal ritual.

Our approach is grounded, compassionate and open to people of any faith or none.

Our principles

What Birchwood holds to be true.

Belong to nature

Human life is one strand within a much older living world. We should move through it with attention, humility and care.

Honour the cycles

Beginnings and endings, growth and decay, grief and joy, darkness and return all belong to the same pattern.

Make room for mystery

Not everything meaningful needs to be explained, measured or reduced. Wonder, intuition, imagination and the unknown deserve a place alongside reason.

Kindness without surrendering truth

Compassion matters, but so do honesty, boundaries and responsibility. Kindness is not the same as submission.

Create meaning deliberately

Ritual, art, story, music, gathering and remembrance can give shape to experiences that modern life often leaves flattened or unnamed.

Leave things gentler

People, animals, places and communities should be treated as though our presence matters, because it does.

Meet the minister

Rev. Nicholas Paul Birch

Ordained Minister, Universal Life Church
Founder, Ministry of Birchwood

Rev. Nicholas Birch is an ordained minister of the Universal Life Church and founder of the Ministry of Birchwood. His approach draws on nature, creativity, ritual and a lifelong fascination with the old symbolic traditions humans have used to make sense of change, loss, belonging and wonder.

Birchwood is grounded rather than dogmatic, personal rather than prescribed, and shaped around the people, places and stories involved. It welcomes people of any faith or none at all.
